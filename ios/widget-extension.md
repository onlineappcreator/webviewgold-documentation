# Widget Extension – WebViewGold for iOS

> **Summary:** Add a Home Screen widget to your iOS WebView app that links users straight into your web app.

[Documentation](../README.md) › [WebViewGold for iOS](README.md) › Widget Extension

**Platform:** iOS · **Category:** Extensions & Plugins · **Online version:** [www.webviewgold.com/docs/iOS/widget-extension/](https://www.webviewgold.com/docs/iOS/widget-extension/)

---

Offer an image widget (SVG or PNG) on the home screen. Activate it following our video guide and configure `widgetRefreshTime` (in minutes) and `widgetfallbackURL` in `Config.swift`. The widget image can be updated from your web app by loading a URL such as `widget://https://example.org/image.svg?user=30`.
